⚠️ Important Notes:

About "Laan van Nieuw Land" and "Verlengde Westerdreef":

These roads may be planned but not yet built, which means they might not exist in the PDOK NWB database yet. The NWB only contains roads that are currently open to traffic.

If the specific road names return 0 results:

  • ✅ Click "All Roads (Bbox)" to see what roads currently exist in the Lelystad Zuid area
  • ✅ Look for existing roads with similar names (Westerdreef without "Verlengde", nearby roads)
  • ✅ Use the sample coordinates from real-road-coordinates-sample.js for your demonstrator
  • ✅ The sample coordinates are geographically accurate for the planned road locations

Troubleshooting: If roads don't appear after fetching, click the Debug button and check the browser console (F12). The test data should always work.
